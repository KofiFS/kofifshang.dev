01Overview

A bright little island, and you are a Soul on it.

You play a small, glossy, jelly-bodied Juicicoran with two simple eyes and a lot of opinions.

You bounce, double jump, hover and ground pound your way around Elyndor: ruined arches, an orchard, flower meadows, a pond, waterfalls and a beach. Above it floats a piece of Aetheron, the sky village, reached by jump pads.

Twenty souls live there, each with a home, a job and a day of their own. Trouble keeps turning up beyond the horizon, so you set sail to deal with it, alone or with up to four friends. Then you come home to your own house for the night.

02Core system

Every fruit is a power. The press makes it juice.

Shake fruit from the trees, pick berries off the bushes, drop them in a juice press, set a bottle on the stone and ground pound it. Drink the juice and its power runs on you: speed, spikes, webs to swing on, lightning steps, bombs, a burrow underground, or a split into several of you.

Mix fruits in one bottle and their powers run together. Some pairs clash, and a clash costs you a heart.

Under the hood this is a power matrix whose fill curves are tuned from a CSV, so balancing twenty-five powers and their combinations is a data change, not a code change.

03World clock

A month on Elyndor is twenty-five days.

Days 1–7Bloom
Days 8–13Blaze
Days 14–19Frost
Days 20–25Storm

The world runs on a persistent clock with day and night. Each season changes the trees, the weather and which fruit grow; rain, fog, snow and thunderstorms roll in. On trade days the market sets up in the plaza, and on some nights a shooting star falls and leaves something rare where it lands.

The souls live on the same clock. Each keeps their own hours, works their own counter, goes home to their own bed, and warms up to you as you run their errands and bring gifts they like.

04Engineering

What it takes to build.

Juicicora is a replicated multiplayer game in Unreal Engine 5.6 C++, which means every system has to work for up to five players at once.

01

Co-op that holds together

Friends join through Steam invites and share one island: exploring together, giving gifts, duelling, and sailing out as one crew. Before the boat leaves, everyone votes on the mission.

Missions earn a rank from F to J, and the rank decides the haul: gems, building materials, fruit, dyes, flowers, cosmetics and Glass Hearts.

02

Enemies with rules, not just health bars

Plant Golems and Floaty Spirits hold the high rock tops; Sand Spirits slide under the dunes and Electric Golems crackle between the rocks. Each has a full set of moves: boulders built from flying pebbles, dashes that blink through you, beams from the sky, cyclones of sand.

Elemental enemies follow counter rules, so water beats fire, and two boss birds, the Gale Mother and the Gale Father, guard the Windward Spire.

03

A living village

Twenty souls each have a home, a job, a schedule and a story told in pieces. Players buy and upgrade houses with gathered sand, stone and wood, take a room at the inn, and keep gems at the bank.

A night in your own bed brings every heart back, which ties the economy, the clock and survival together.

04

Making collaboration cheap

Tuning lives in data where it can, like the power matrix’s CSV-driven curves. A one-click setup script writes a full log, so a collaborator’s broken setup can be diagnosed from a single file.
