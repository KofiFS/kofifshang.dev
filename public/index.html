Clutch

The game clip recorder you can tell what to clip. Light like NVIDIA’s recorder, social like Medal, with no ads you can’t turn off.

Status
Out now · v0.1.8
Platform
Windows 10 and 11
Stack
Electron, React, TypeScript, FFmpeg
License
GPL-3.0, free
Hype momentsClips when your friends get loud. No AI.
Medal, OBS and NVIDIAOld clips read in place, nothing copied.
01

An always-on replay buffer

One FFmpeg process encodes on the GPU into two-second segments. Saving a clip stitches them by stream copy, so it is instant and never re-encodes.

02

Clips that make themselves

Hype and conversation triggers run locally with no AI. If you want, describe what to keep in plain English and Clutch watches for it.

03

Your library, not a new silo

Existing Medal, OBS and NVIDIA clips show up where they already are. A 9:16 editor, Discord posting and scheduled YouTube uploads are built in.
