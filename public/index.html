Clutch

Out now · free

The game clip recorder you can tell what to clip. An always-on replay buffer that is light like NVIDIA's recorder and social like Medal, with no ads you cannot turn off. It reads your existing Medal, OBS and NVIDIA clips in place, so nothing is copied, moved or deleted.

Electron, React and TypeScript, with GPU encoding through NVENC, AMF and Quick Sync. Six releases in five days. Windows 10 and 11.

Download for Windows Source on GitHub
  • Clip hotkey saves the seconds before and after, and you can change the length mid-game
  • Clips that make themselves when you or your friends suddenly get loud
  • Describe what to clip in plain English, like "my kills, never menus"
  • Per-game profiles and automatic game detection
  • Built-in editor with 9:16 crops for Shorts and TikTok
  • Posts to Discord and uploads to YouTube, now or scheduled
