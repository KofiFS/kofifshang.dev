Clutch

The game clip recorder you can tell what to clip. An always-on replay buffer that is light like NVIDIA's recorder and social like Medal, with no ads you cannot turn off. Describe what you want kept in plain English, or let it clip itself when you and your friends suddenly get loud. It reads your existing Medal, OBS and NVIDIA clips in place, so nothing is copied, moved or deleted.

  • Clip hotkey saves the seconds before and after, adjustable mid-game
  • Automatic clipping on hype moments and conversation, no AI needed
  • Per-game profiles and automatic game detection
  • Built-in 9:16 editor for Shorts and TikTok
  • Posts to Discord, uploads to YouTube on a schedule
  • GPU encoding through NVENC, AMF and Quick Sync

Electron, React and TypeScript · GPL-3.0 · Windows 10 and 11 · six releases in five days

Juicicora

A cosy co-op adventure for up to four players. You are a small glossy creature exploring hand-painted meadows, chalk cliffs and floating islands, dashing and wall-jumping between them, running missions and mini-games, and mixing fruit power-ups at an alchemy table. Befriend the villagers, travel by portal, and collect outfits worth showing off.

  • Four-player online co-op with missions and mini-games
  • Elemental enemies with counter rules, bosses and duels
  • A persistent world clock with day and night
  • Collectible cosmetics, emotes and fruit power-ups
  • Portals between hand-built worlds
  • Built on Unreal Engine 5.6 in C++

Unreal Engine 5.6 · C++ · replicated multiplayer · the deepest systems work I have going
